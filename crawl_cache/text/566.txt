Phase I Small Team applications request full proposals by 4/28/2026. Phase II Large Team applications request letters of intent by 4/28/2026. Applicant institutions may submit one application as the lead institution per focus area for Phase I and Phase II applications combined. There are a total of 99 focus areas found on pages 12 through 53 of the solicitation. Interested applicants are encouraged to indicate their targeted focus area and partner institution(s) (DOE/NNSA National Laboratory/Scientific User Facility or industry partner) in the notification of intent to submit form. If you are interested in developing a proposal in response to this funding opportunity, please complete a notification of intent to submit by 03/25/2026 . For more information regarding the internal selection process, visit https://research.unl.edu/fundingopps/active-limited-submission-competitions/ .

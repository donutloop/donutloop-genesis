Currently, each particle accelerator facility requires tailored approaches to access and prepare data for AI model applications. This limits the generalizability and reusability of insights across different facilities. To overcome these challenges, this project will establish AI-ready data standards to streamline processes and enhance collaboration across DOE/SC particle accelerator facilities. This project is the crucial first step toward developing a generalized optimization/controls ecosystem, digital twin, and particle accelerator foundation model, that can be trained on diverse operational data from across our facilities. Preparing QCD Data for Foundation Models This effort will create a transferable framework for the U.S. Department of Energy's Office of Science facilities, including Jefferson Lab's Continuous Electron Beam Accelerator Facility and the future Electron-Ion Collider, that builds towards the development of AI models that can seamlessly span experimental and theoretical inputs, thus accelerating progress towards the science laid out in the 2023 Long Range Plan for Nuclear Science .

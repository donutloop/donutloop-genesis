The Genesis Mission is a national initiative by the Department of Energy to use AI to accelerate advancements in key scientific fields and address bottlenecks in the discovery and development of new technologies. One key challenge the Genesis Mission aims to confront is understanding and predicting the availability of water for expanding production and utilization of energy, as well as protecting the nation’s health and security. Brown is a Co-PI in a joint project with Oak Ridge National Laboratory to build AI foundation models that predict and test water-for-energy conditions in the Tennessee Valley Authority region. Brown’s project is one of seven Genesis Mission awards involving Georgia Tech researchers. The projects range in scope from advanced biomanufacturing to development of fusion power plant systems. Brown’s award underscores the role of EPIcenter faculty affiliates in energy innovation research and strengthens Georgia Tech’s national #1 ranking in energy research.

Now, PsiQuantum builds thousands of wafers of quantum chips in a tier-1 foundry, operates cryogenic cabinets designed to support hundreds of chips, advances fault-tolerant algorithm development to solve critical problems across industries, and is developing a modular quantum computing platform designed to increase capability and scale. Each milestone brings us closer to delivering commercially useful quantum computing.

Approach: Aligned with the Genesis OPAL Project , agentic AI leverages the Frontier exascale supercomputer for real-time analyses of data from ORNL’s autonomous Advanced Plant Phenotyping Laboratory (APPL), exploring ways to enhance how roots “mine” dilute critical minerals from soils. Impacts: Agentic AI monitors, interprets, and recommends on-the-fly adjustments to experiments to accelerate adaptive learning for bioengineering. Reduces analysis time of APPL imaging from 168 hours to <1 minute. Enables adaptive workflows and smart experiments for bioengineering (enhancing plant uptake of critical minerals).

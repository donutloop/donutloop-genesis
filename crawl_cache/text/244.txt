The Genesis Mission unites DOE National Labs, industry, academia, and more to harness AI for breakthroughs in energy dominance, discovery science, and national security. Inside the Genesis Mission The Genesis Mission is turning vision into action. Building a national AI-for-science ecosystem. Advancing new partnerships. Developing cutting-edge capabilities. All to accelerate scientific discovery. Addressing National Science and Technology Challenges in Key Areas DOE has identified national science and technology challenges to accelerate U.S. innovation and leadership.

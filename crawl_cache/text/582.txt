This effort signals a significant federal commitment to accelerating discovery across energy, materials, nuclear security, and related domains, and it is likely to shape research priorities and funding opportunities across the DOE ecosystem in the coming years. I encourage you to review the announcement and begin thinking about how your faculty, centers, and programs may align with the emerging directions associated with Genesis. Large, coordinated efforts that connect fundamental research to deployment-scale applications — particularly those leveraging AI-enabled discovery and advanced computing — are expected to be central to this initiative. Our office will monitor developments closely and share updates as specific engagement opportunities, partnerships, and solicitations become clearer. In the meantime, if you have teams whose work strongly aligns with these areas, please let us know so we can ensure the university is well positioned.

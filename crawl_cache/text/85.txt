GF Labs builds foundational technologies years ahead of demand. Advanced packaging Our research advances the next generation of packaging architectures from chiplet integration to high-density interconnects that extend the performance, density and efficiency of GF’s technologies. As compute demands outpace what monolithic scaling can deliver, packaging is becoming where system performance is won. Learn more : Advanced packaging Quantum GF Labs is building the architectures, materials and partner ecosystem required to position GF for leadership in the emerging quantum market. Our work establishes foundational capabilities from spin-qubit devices to cryogenic integration that will underpin commercial quantum systems later this decade. Silicon photonics Building on our proven silicon photonics technologies, GF Labs helps pave the way for next‑generation applications in data center interconnects, high‑speed communications and compact LiDAR systems. Our research is paving the way for the transition from pluggables to co-packaged optics to keep pace with the demands of hyperscale data centers.

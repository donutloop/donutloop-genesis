National Laboratories Our 17 National Laboratories are home to an unparalleled concentration of world-class scientists, engineers, and researchers. To support the Genesis Mission, the Labs’ expertise and resources are organized around teams that form the foundation of the world’s most powerful scientific platform. Data The data team transforms DOE and NNSA scientific data into secure, connected, high-value resources for accelerated discovery and national security. Learn more . Infrastructure The infrastructure team integrates advanced computing, cloud technologies, and DOE and NNSA research facilities to establish the infrastructure for the American Science and Security Platform. Learn more . Models The models team delivers and transforms powerful AI models and tools into active collaborators—developing hypotheses, running simulations, and executing experiments to drive scientific breakthroughs. Learn more . National Science and Technology Challenges The National Challenges team harnesses AI to address national science and technology priorities to deliver real-world impact on our nation’s most pressing challenges. Learn more . Public-Private Partnerships The public-private partnerships team connects U.S. government resources with industry and academia to accelerate the productivity and impact of American science and engineering. Learn more . The Genesis Mission Consortium The Genesis Mission Consortium unites the nation’s leading science and technology experts to solve critical challenges at unprecedented speed by connecting leading industry, academic, nonprofit, and philanthropic organizations with DOE and the National Laboratories and their resources. The consortium will use working groups to seed and facilitate strategic, high-impact partnerships in: AI Model Development and Validation Data Integration and Standards High-Performance Computing and Cloud Infrastructure Robotics and Automation Workforce Development and Training Individuals and entities interested in the consortium can subscribe to Genesis Mission Consortium updates and learn about future engagement opportunities.

The program provides targeted funding to initiate joint research projects, build sustained collaborations, pursue joint appointments and facilitate access to specialized expertise, facilities and research capabilities. In particular, collaborations with Idaho National Laboratory (INL) and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL) are encouraged because of their geographic proximity to Boise State and the potential to develop stronger, long-term research connections. Proposals involving other national labs will also be considered when they demonstrate a compelling opportunity to advance Boise State’s research capacity and establish a productive, sustainable collaboration. In addition, priority consideration will be given to proposals that address one or more of the following focus areas: Critical Minerals Quantum Information Science Advanced Manufacturing Genesis Mission Cybersecurity Quantum-AI Successful proposals should demonstrate the potential for the seed investment to lead to a sustained research relationship, future external funding, expanded use of national laboratory expertise or facilities, joint appointments, or other longer-term collaborative outcomes. Awards: Two tracks available Track 1: Travel support grants Amount and duration: Up to $4,000 for the duration of 6 months. Anticipated awards: Up to 5 awards (distributed between INL and other national labs). Goals: Travel Support Grants are intended to initiate or strengthen engagement with national laboratory researchers and capabilities. Funds may support activities such as meeting with new or prospective collaborators, participating in research workshops or planning meetings, visiting national laboratory facilities and accessing specialized tools, instruments, user facilities, recharge centers or other research capabilities. Applicants should demonstrate how the proposed activities will advance a national laboratory collaboration, such as development of a joint research project, future Collaborative Seed Grant, external funding proposal, publication, joint appointment or other sustained collaborative activity. Target Submission Date: September 15, 2026. Applications received by September 15 will receive priority consideration. Applications received after the target date will be reviewed on a rolling basis until all available awards have been made. Use of Funds: Funds may be used for expenses directly related to developing or strengthening the national laboratory collaboration, including: Travel to the national laboratory; Collaborative meetings or workshops; User facility or recharge costs; Instrument or tool access and other expenses necessary to advance the proposed engagement. Track 2: Collaborative seed grants Amount and duration: Up to $30,000 with an end date of June 30, 2027. Anticipated awards: Up to 4 awards are anticipated (3 aligned with INL, and 1 aligned with PNNL or another national lab). Goal: Small funding to support initial grant development, active collaborations and joint appointments. Metrics for success: The primary intended outcomes are proposal submissions and publications. Deadline: Submissions are due Dec. 1, 2026. A total of four awards will be accepted. Use of Funds: Funds may be used for research supplies and materials; faculty, staff, student salary support; facility, instrument, or recharge center costs and other research expenses directly related to the collaborative project. Eligibility Eligibility: The program is open to Boise State faculty. Priority consideration will be given to faculty establishing new collaborations. Seed grant requirement: Proposals for the Collaborative Seed Grant require a formal letter of support from the national lab collaborator. Proposal format and requirements Proposals must be submitted as a single PDF and include the following items: Title/Cover page: Including the names, contact information, and departments of the PI, co-PIs, and the collaborating national lab partner(s). Project narrative: Not to exceed two pages for travel support and four pages for seed grants. Project Overview: Describe the research goals, rationale, and significance of the proposed activity or collaboration. Identify and describe the project’s alignment with one or more of the program’s priority focus areas, as applicable. Approach: Describe the proposed objectives, activities, timeline, and logistics (such as travel to specific user facilities or tool usage). Outcomes: Outline the anticipated outcomes and success metrics. Describe how the proposed activities could lead to continued collaboration, including targeted external funding opportunities, joint publications, joint appointments, expanded use of national lab capabilities, or other longer-term collaborative outcomes. Letter of support (seed grants only): Include a letter of support from the national lab collaborator confirming their engagement in and commitment to the proposed project. Budget and budget justification: Provide a detailed breakdown of anticipated costs (travel, supplies, effort, student support and other applicable expenses). Review and selection Proposals will be evaluated based on the quality and feasibility of the proposed project, alignment with the program’s priority focus areas, strength of the national lab collaboration, and potential for the investment to lead to sustained research outcomes. Reviewers will consider: Project quality and feasibility – Clear goals, a well-defined approach, and a realistic plan for carrying out the proposed project. Alignment with priority focus areas – The extent to which the project aligns with one or more of the priority research areas. Strength of collaboration – The potential to establish or strengthen a collaboration with the national lab partner. Potential for follow-on outcomes – The likelihood that the project will lead to external funding, joint publications, joint appointments, expanded use of national lab expertise or facilities, or other sustained collaborative outcomes.

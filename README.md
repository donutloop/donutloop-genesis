The Genesis Mission is a major U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) initiative launched to integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI), quantum computing, and high-performance supercomputing directly into scientific research. 

---

## Presentation Details

* **Title:** Genesis Mission and HEP - LHC
* **File:** `Genesis Mission and HEP - LHC.pdf`

### Event & Location
* **Event:** [U.S. ATLAS Institutional Board Meeting](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1662511/?view=indico_weeks_view)
* **When:** Wednesday, March 18, 2026, from 13:00 to 14:00 (US Eastern Time)
* **Where:** Held remotely via Zoom (Hosted via CERN’s Indico system — Event ID: 1662511, Contribution ID: 6989580)

### Participants & Key Figures
* **Presenter:** Jeremy Love (Program Manager, U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science - High Energy Physics / DOE-HEP)
* **Attendees / Organizers:** Members of the U.S. ATLAS Institutional Board, including researchers, physicists, and institutional representatives involved in the [ATLAS experiment at CERN](https://atlas.cern/Discover/Collaboration).

> **Note:** ATLAS is one of the largest collaborative efforts ever attempted in science.

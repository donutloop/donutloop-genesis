## 2. Superconducting Transmon Hardware & Fab-1 Foundry Architecture
- **Fab-1 Cleanroom Foundry (Fremont, CA):**
  - **200mm Silicon/Sapphire Wafer Line:** In-house fabrication facility utilizing advanced lithography, electron-beam evaporation, anisotropic etching, and superconducting metal deposition (Aluminum, Niobium, Tantalum).
  - **3D Packaging & Superconducting TSVs:** Implements Through-Silicon Vias (TSVs), 3D multi-chip interposers, and indium bump bonding to route control signals vertically from cryogenic wiring to the QPU plane without thermal crosstalk.
  - **High Yield & Modular Tileability:** Enables high-yield production of modular 84-qubit tiles stitched together for multi-chip scaling.
- **Transmon Qubit Physics & Tunable Couplers:**
  - **Fixed & Tunable Transmon Qubits:** Leverages Josephson junction transmon circuits protected against charge noise with $E_J / E_C \gg 1$.
  - **Square Lattice Connectivity:** Employs a 2D square grid topology with active flux-tunable couplers, enabling fast Controlled-Phase (CZ) and iSWAP entangling gates with sub-50 ns gate durations.
- **Novera™ 9-Qubit On-Premises System:**
  - **Architecture:** Features 9 3D-integrated transmon qubits arranged in a 3x3 grid with 5 tunable couplers, packaged in a compact dilution refrigerator insert.
  - **Target Customers:** Government laboratories, supercomputing centers (PSC), and academic research groups establishing local quantum-classical testbeds.

## 3. Modular Multi-Chip Scaling & 3D Packaging (Ankaa & Cepheus)
- **Ankaa™ 84-Qubit Single-Chip Platform:**
  - **Topology & Speed:** 84-qubit processor on a square lattice with flux-tunable couplers, delivering 2.5x faster gate speeds compared to Aspen-M.
  - **Fidelity Milestones:** Achieves 98.0%+ median two-qubit gate fidelity on Ankaa-1 and Ankaa-2, targeting 99.5%+ two-qubit gate fidelity on Ankaa-3.
- **Cepheus™ 336-Qubit Multi-Chip Modular Architecture:**
  - **Tiled Multi-Chip Scaling:** Stitches four 84-qubit Ankaa dies together using 3D-integrated superconducting interposer chips and inter-chip flux couplers.
  - **Crosstalk Mitigation:** Multi-chip design isolates control line crosstalk, maintaining high gate fidelity across multi-die module boundaries.

## 4. Quantum Cloud Services (QCS™), Quil & PyQuil Software
- **QCS™ Cloud Platform:**
  - **Co-Located Ultra-Low Latency:** Integrates classical Linux compute servers in direct physical proximity to dilution refrigerators, achieving sub-millisecond QPU-classical loop latency.
  - **Multi-Cloud Integrations:** Native QCS integration accessible via AWS Braket, Strangeworks, Azure Quantum, and direct Rigetti QCS cloud endpoints.
- **Quil™ & PyQuil Software Ecosystem:**
  - **Quil™ Instruction Set:** Open-source Quantum Instruction Language supporting active qubit reset, memory allocation, and parametric circuit execution.
  - **PyQuil & Quilc Compiler:** Python SDK (PyQuil) coupled with Quilc optimizing compiler to perform pulse-level gate synthesis, cross-talk reduction, and circuit optimization.

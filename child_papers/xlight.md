## 2. Free-Electron Laser (FEL) & Energy Recovery Linac (ERL) Architecture
- **Particle Accelerator Core:**
  - **Relativistic Electron Bunches:** Accelerates electron bunches to multi-hundred MeV energies using high-gradient superconducting cavities operating at 2 Kelvin.
  - **High-Field Undulators:** Passes electron bunches through magnetic undulator arrays, triggering coherent micro-bunching and stimulated emission of EUV photons.
- **Energy Recovery Linac (ERL) Efficiency:**
  - **Deceleration & Power Recovery:** Recovers kinetic energy from spent electrons by decelerating them through the SRF cavities prior to beam dump, reducing power consumption by up to 90%.

## 3. Sub-2nm Semiconductor Lithography & High-NA EUV Wavelength Control
- **Elimination of Liquid Tin Debris & Downtime:**
  - **Clean Photon Generation:** Unlike laser-produced plasma (LPP) systems that vaporize molten tin droplets, xLight's FEL generates clean light in a high-vacuum beam pipe, eliminating collector mirror tin contamination and fab downtime.
- **High-NA & Hyper-NA Scanner Compatibility:**
  - Wavelength-tunable light source fully compatible with High-NA (0.55 NA) and Hyper-NA scanner optical trains for patterning sub-2nm and 1.4nm transistor gates.

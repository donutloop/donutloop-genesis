## 2. 3D Optical Tweezer Arrays & Ytterbium-171 Nuclear Spin Qubits
- **3D Optical Tweezer Lattices:**
  - **Spatial Light Modulator (SLM) Optics:** Uses high-resolution SLMs and high-NA optical lenses to generate arrays of 1,225 optical micro-traps holding individual neutral atoms in a 3D grid topology.
  - **Wireless Optical Control:** Eliminates physical wiring, lithographic interconnects, and cryogenic microwave lines by steering laser beams to address qubits optically.
- **Nuclear Spin-1/2 Qubits ($^{171}\text{Yb}$):**
  - **Hyper-Fine Decoupling:** Encodes qubits into the $F=1/2$ nuclear spin ground states of alkaline-earth-like Ytterbium-171, rendering qubits naturally insensitive to stray electric fields and ambient magnetic noise.
  - **World-Record Coherence ($T_2 > 40\text{ s}$):** Demonstrates atomic nuclear spin coherence times $T_2 > 40\text{ seconds}$ without active dynamical decoupling.

---

## 3. Rydberg Entangling Gates & Optical Clock Laser Control
- **Rydberg Blockade Multi-Qubit Gates:**
  - **Rydberg State Excitation:** Drives neutral atoms to high principal quantum number Rydberg states ($n > 70$) using ultra-narrow linewidth $578\text{ nm}$ optical clock lasers, inducing dipole blockade for ultra-fast two-qubit CZ gates.
- **Qubit Recycling & Mid-Circuit Measurement:**
  - **Non-Destructive Readout:** Uses resonance fluorescence for non-destructive state measurement and active atomic re-trapping, enabling active error correction loops during execution.

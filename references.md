# Genesis Mission Overview

<img width="2006" height="1121" alt="Screenshot From 2026-07-26 10-50-55" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d46729cd-904e-4d4e-8fd5-5853f77ac55f" />

The **Genesis Mission** is a major U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) initiative designed to integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI), quantum computing, and high-performance supercomputing directly into scientific research.

## 3. Quantum Leadership & CHIPS Act Initiatives

* **Official Announcement:** [Department of Commerce $2 Billion Letters of Intent Announcement (NIST)](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/05/department-commerce-announces-letters-intent-9-companies-2-billion)
* **DOE Quantum Computing Initiative:** [Energy Department $2 Billion Fault-Tolerant Quantum Initiative](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/energy-department-announces-initiative-create-and-deploy-worlds-first)

### Quantum Foundries & Manufacturing Infrastructure
* [GlobalFoundries](https://gf.com/gf-labs): **$375 Million** planned funding to establish a secure, domestic quantum foundry across multiple modalities.
* [IBM](https://research.ibm.com/blog/ibm-us-genesis-mission-quantum-ai): **$1 Billion** planned funding to establish a new quantum foundry subsidiary for quantum-grade superconducting wafers.

### Quantum Computing & Modality Developers
* [Atom Computing](https://atom-computing.com/hardware): **$100 Million** planned funding for scaling neutral-atom quantum computing hardware and system integration.
* [Diraq](https://diraq.com/solutions): **Up to $38 Million** planned funding to develop and scale silicon spin quantum logic units and qubit arrays.
* [D-Wave](https://www.dwavesys.com/solutions/quantum-computing-for-science/): **$100 Million** planned funding to advance annealing and gate-model superconducting quantum computing systems.
* [Infleqtion](https://www.infleqtion.com/sqale): **$100 Million** planned funding for large-scale neutral-atom architectures, high-powered optical systems, and error correction (Secured 3 DOE Genesis Mission Projects).
* [PsiQuantum](https://psiquantum.com/technology): **$100 Million** planned funding to address photonic quantum computing challenges, detectors, and low-loss packaging.
* [Quantinuum](https://www.quantinuum.com/hardware/h-series): **$100 Million** planned funding to solve manufacturing bottlenecks for trapped-ion-based fault-tolerant quantum computers.
* [Rigetti Computing](https://www.rigetti.com/systems): **Up to $100 Million** planned funding for miniaturized readout electronics and next-generation superconducting architectures.

* [NSF 26-512: Unlocking Dataset Value for AI-Enabled Scientific Discovery (AI Datasets) — Program Solicitation](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/ai-datasets-unlocking-dataset-value-ai-enabled-scientific-discovery/nsf26-512/solicitation) - Full text of the **NSF 26-512** *Program Solicitation* (solicitation document **posted July 21, 2026** and served as "the current version"; the funding instrument behind the already-indexed *AI Datasets* opportunity page), fetched and verified in this round. **Issuing structure**: a cross-directorate program run jointly by the NSF **Directorate for Computer and Information Science and Engineering (CISE)**, **Directorate for Biological Sciences**, **Directorate for Engineering**, **Directorate for Geosciences**, **Directorate for Mathematical and Physical Sciences** and **Directorate for Technology, Innovation and Partnerships (TIP)** together with the **Office of Research Infrastructure**; program contact `aidatasets@nsf.gov`; Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance numbers **47.041, 47.049, 47.050, 47.070, 47.074, 47.084**. **Programme goal**: advance scientific-community datasets so AI and other methods can unlock discovery from data collected for other purposes, via (1) AI-based **feature extraction, metadata generation and integration of multiple datasets**, (2) **robust data pipelines** for automated analysis of existing datasets, and (3) **augmentation and harmonization** of existing datasets for AI data pipelines — the solicitation records that many scientific datasets are not findable, accessible or interoperable and lack the integrity, data descriptors and pipelines required by large-scale AI applications, that AI systems increasingly become *the initial consumer* of datasets, and that many datasets lack identified owners or sufficiently organized scientific communities, while research to collect **de novo** datasets is explicitly **out of scope** and stays with the individual scientific directorates. **Award structure**: anticipated funding **$60,000,000–$100,000,000** (subject to the availability of funds) across **25 to 50 awards** as standard grants or cooperative agreements — **Impact awards** up to 3 years and a maximum **$2,000,000** (approximately 10–20), **Flagship awards** up to 3 years and a maximum **$5,000,000** (approximately 5–10, for challenges not achievable without more substantial resources), and **Planning grants** up to **$200,000** (approximately 10–20, for community planning and early-stage AI-data challenges); **Full Proposal deadline November 04, 2026** and the **first Wednesday in November, annually thereafter**, 5 p.m. submitting organization's local time; no letters of intent or preliminary proposals required. **Genesis Mission coupling**: the program is stated to be aligned with the **America's AI Action Plan** — world-class AI-ready scientific datasets and public release of high-quality datasets from prior federally funded research being named priorities — and to support the **Genesis Mission**, described as building "an integrated platform to harness scientific datasets to train scientific models, create AI agents to test new hypotheses, automate research workflows and accelerate scientific breakthroughs"; proposals are encouraged to leverage **NSF data platforms**, the **NSF Integrated Data Systems and Services (IDSS)** program, the **NSF-led National AI Research Resource (NAIRR)**, the **Genesis Mission platform** or other national infrastructure, and NSF states it is open to **philanthropy, private industry and non-profit sector partnerships** to support additional proposals or collaborative opportunities. **Mandatory proposal content**: dataset **governance** (access, retirement policies, community contribution process) and **dataset security and integrity** (provenance, trust, quality, privacy, authenticity, sensitive datasets, research-security issues); Project Description sections covering *Vision and community benefit for dataset enhancement*, *Research plan*, *Dataset governance and integrity* and *Dataset availability and infrastructure*, plus an additional **Flagship Justification** section for Flagship proposals; proposal titles must begin with **"AIDatasets:Impact"**, **"AIDatasets:Flagship"** or **"AIDatasets:Planning"** followed by a colon and the project title, and the Project Summary must close with a prioritized **`Keywords:`** line of 2–5 keywords separated by semicolons. **Eligibility**: two- and four-year **Institutions of Higher Education** including community colleges, **non-profit non-academic organizations** (independent museums, observatories, research laboratories, professional societies), **U.S.-based for-profit organizations** including small businesses, **State and Local Governments**, **Tribal Nations** recognized under the Federally Recognized Indian Tribe List Act of 1994 (25 U.S.C. §§ 5130–5131), and **other Federal Agencies and FFRDCs** under PAPPG Chapter I.E.2; **no restrictions or limits** on who may serve as PI or on proposals per organization or per PI; multi-organizational proposals permitted with a single lead organization and all others as subawards; an active **UEI through SAM.gov** is required; **inclusion of voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited** and indirect-cost limitations are listed as not applicable. **Review**: the two National Science Board merit review criteria (**Intellectual Merit**, **Broader Impacts**) plus additional solicitation-specific criteria — size/commitment/potential impact/breadth and outreach plan of the benefiting communities, novelty and transformative potential of the dataset enhancements, dataset security/integrity/governance including the community contribution process and sustainment plan, viability and ease of adoption by a target community, and for Flagship proposals the justification for the increased funding level; **panel review** following an initial compliance check, an NSF Program Officer review and three or more external experts as ad hoc reviewers, panelists or both. Citing **Executive Order 14332** (*Improving Oversight of Federal Grantmaking*), the solicitation notes that all else being equal after merit review NSF **may give preference to institutions with lower indirect cost rates** and that awards may include **termination for convenience** provisions. **Measures of success**: datasets expanded or established and new technologies or techniques for leveraged AI and AI-guided exploration (Discovery and Innovation), undergraduates and graduate students trained in AI use of scientific datasets (STEM Workforce and Education), and new built infrastructure that leverages existing datasets plus research facility usage (Research Infrastructure); awardees are expected to engage in **bi-annual virtual meetings** and to budget for in-person meetings such as an annual PI meeting, the **NSF NAIRR community meeting** or a discipline-specific community meeting.
